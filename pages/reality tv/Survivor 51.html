Survivor 51



Episode 1

Honestly this was an amazing premiere. I really think that when they said that they are turning over a new leaf with the open era that they kind of want to go back to their roots a little bit. I cannot believe that we got a hidden idol that you do not need to do something silly for and you do not even have to risk your vote. I am curious to see what they will do with the whole Exile Island thing and I feel really bad for Louis who has just been stranded out there and then shredded his hands to try and get an idol just for him to not get it. The tribal council might have been the best first council ever, I do not think that there has been a first council that went live before, and I am so glad that we have bigger tribes again. There are only so many things that can happen with six people but when you bump it up to 10 there is so much more uncertainty.

Episode 2

Once again it seems that Survivor 51 will be delivering with very interesting episodes! I am very excited to see where this season goes after just two episodes lmao. For the first time in 51 seasons someone has tried to vote for themself and subsequently vote themself out of the game. Rob put his name down until the producer told him he could not do that, I cannot imagine getting on a show like this and not knowing the rules. His plan barely made any sense in the first place and I do not know how he was so confident in the other guy getting voted out unamiously. I guess we will see this week what ends up happening, I mean we are also bound for some sort of twist soonish!

Episode 3

Well there goes Rob. I did not have high expectations for him after the last episode but what he has been doing online after has been quite odd. Not surprisingly people have been calling him out for quitting for no reason and he has been trying to say that it was warranted or something. I think that he might just be a masochist who gets off to all of the attention that he has been getting for it. Other than that the whole muntany thing was very exciting and I really want to know if the guy truly missed the boat or if he was just playing it up for tv. Patt going out was a little sad because she was positioned very well in the game and kind of just got caught in the crossfire of everything, but overall another very good episode!

Episode 4

I need to start typing these a little sooner after the episode airs because after a couple days how I feel about it definitely goes away a little bit. Of course they went back to three tribes for no reason, they are like heroin addicts for the three tribe format lmao. Jenna got absolutely screwed by the tribe swap, once again being put on a tribe with Devin, she really could not escape that man. I really hope that this season stays unspoiled unlike 50, which it seems to have done so far. I would imagine after everything that happened with Aubry last season that they made people keep their mouths shut much much more.

Episode 5

Probably one of the best tribal councils that I have ever seen. To be honest I have no clue how Maggie was able to convice Lewis and Kilby to do it and it is even harder to believe that it even worked. I thought for sure that they were going to go to rocks and Cristian was gonna go home because of his edit so far but they decided to send the girl who I cannot remember her name home instead. I doubt that I will ever see anything like that again mainly because you need the right kind of players to do it. If Kilby was not so willing to go to rocks then the others would not have flipped. I mean going to rocks for an ally when you still have the numbers anyways is a little silly but I guess that some people are willing to die for their allies. I really do not care about the rest of the epsiode and to be honest it does not seem like anyone else does as well lmao.
