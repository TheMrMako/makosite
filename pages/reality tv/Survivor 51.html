Survivor 51



Episode 1

Honestly this was an amazing premiere. I really think that when they said that they are turning over a new leaf with the open era that they kind of want to go back to their roots a little bit. I cannot believe that we got a hidden idol that you do not need to do something silly for and you do not even have to risk your vote. I am curious to see what they will do with the whole Exile Island thing and I feel really bad for Louis who has just been stranded out there and then shredded his hands to try and get an idol just for him to not get it. The tribal council might have been the best first council ever, I do not think that there has been a first council that went live before, and I am so glad that we have bigger tribes again. There are only so many things that can happen with six people but when you bump it up to 10 there is so much more uncertainty.
